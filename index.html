COMPLETED PROJECT

Metallic Records

Business Website Package — Metallic Records

WebForge Studio handled the Metallic Records project as a Business Website Package, developing a professional and responsive digital platform around the client's brand, objectives, and requirements. We structured the website with clear navigation and organised sections for the brand, artists, music, services, gallery, and contact experience.

To meet the specific needs of the project, we extended the standard business website package with additional features and functionality, including music-focused content, digital-store functionality, custom content structure, and interactive website elements. We then refined the interface for mobile, tablet, and desktop, implemented the required functionality and integration points, tested the navigation and overall user experience, and completed the project for online delivery.

Package: Business Website
