1. Purpose of This Policy

This Data Retention & Deletion Policy explains how WebForge Studio may retain, manage, store, and delete information obtained through its website, services, client projects, enquiries, and business operations.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that information is retained only for appropriate business, operational, security, accounting, contractual, or legal purposes and is deleted or disposed of when it is no longer reasonably required, subject to applicable law.

2. Scope

This policy applies to information that WebForge Studio may hold in connection with its website visitors, prospective clients, existing clients, suppliers, service providers, enquiries, transactions, and completed or ongoing projects.

It applies to information stored in electronic systems, business records, communications, project files, and other reasonably controlled business environments.

3. Types of Information That May Be Retained

Depending on the circumstances, WebForge Studio may retain information such as contact details, enquiry information, project information, quotations, invoices, payment records, communications, technical records, website-related information, and information required to provide or support services.

The type and amount of information retained will depend on the nature of the relationship and the services involved.

4. Retention Principles

WebForge Studio aims to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or for another lawful purpose.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information, the applicable agreement, legal requirements, business requirements, security considerations, and whether an active project or dispute exists.

5. Personal Information

Personal information will be handled in accordance with the WebForge Studio Privacy Policy and applicable South African privacy and data protection requirements.

Personal information will not be retained indefinitely merely because it has been collected.

6. Client and Project Records

WebForge Studio may retain client and project records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, support completed work, resolve disputes, meet contractual obligations, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Project records may include quotations, agreements, specifications, communications, invoices, technical information, deliverables, and other information reasonably connected to the project.

7. Enquiries and Prospective Clients

Information provided during an enquiry may be retained for a reasonable period so that WebForge Studio can respond to enquiries, follow up on potential work, maintain appropriate business records, or prevent repeated misuse of communication channels.

Where the information is no longer reasonably required and there is no lawful reason to retain it, it may be deleted or securely disposed of.

8. Contracts and Agreements

WebForge Studio may retain copies of contracts, terms, agreements, approvals, and other contractual records for the period reasonably required to establish, exercise, or defend contractual rights and obligations.

Records may also be retained where necessary to comply with legal, accounting, tax, regulatory, or dispute-resolution requirements.

9. Financial and Payment Records

Invoices, payment confirmations, transaction records, refunds, and other financial information may need to be retained for accounting, tax, financial management, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal purposes.

Such information may therefore be retained for longer than ordinary enquiry or communication records where applicable requirements require it.

10. Communications

WebForge Studio may retain relevant emails, messages, enquiries, and other business communications where reasonably necessary to provide services, document instructions or approvals, resolve disputes, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations.

Unnecessary communications may be deleted or securely disposed of when they are no longer reasonably required.

11. Technical and Security Records

WebForge Studio may retain technical information, system records, security logs, access records, or other operational information where reasonably necessary to maintain security, investigate incidents, prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems, or protect its services and users.

Such records will be retained only for a period reasonably appropriate to the relevant purpose and applicable requirements.

12. Website and Analytics Information

Information relating to website usage, analytics, cookies, and similar technologies may be retained in accordance with the applicable purposes described in the WebForge Studio Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Retention may vary depending on the technology or third-party service involved.

13. Legal and Regulatory Requirements

WebForge Studio may retain information where retention is required or reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, lawful requests, accounting requirements, tax obligations, or other legal responsibilities.

Information retained for such purposes may be kept for the period required by the relevant obligation.

14. Disputes and Legal Claims

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal rights, investigate complaints, resolve disputes, or address potential claims.

This may apply even where the information would otherwise have been scheduled for deletion.

15. Data Minimisation

WebForge Studio aims to avoid retaining unnecessary information and seeks to limit retained information to what is reasonably relevant to the applicable business, contractual, operational, security, or legal purpose.

Where information is no longer reasonably required, WebForge Studio may delete, anonymise, or securely dispose of it where appropriate.

16. Retention Periods

WebForge Studio does not apply one universal retention period to all information. Different types of information may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose and the circumstances in which they were collected.

Retention periods may be influenced by contractual requirements, operational needs, security considerations, accounting and tax obligations, legal requirements, and legitimate business purposes.

17. Completed Projects

Information relating to completed projects may be retained after delivery where reasonably necessary for maintenance, support, warranty-related matters, record keeping, legal protection, accounting, or future communication concerning the project.

Retention of project information does not necessarily mean that all project materials will be kept indefinitely.

18. Inactive Client Information

Where a client relationship becomes inactive, WebForge Studio may review the information associated with that relationship and retain only information that remains reasonably necessary for legitimate business, contractual, legal, financial, security, or other lawful purposes.

19. Deletion Requests

Where applicable, an individual or client may request that certain personal information be deleted or removed from WebForge Studio's records.

Requests will be considered in accordance with applicable law and WebForge Studio's legal, contractual, security, accounting, and operational obligations.

20. Limits on Deletion

WebForge Studio may be unable to immediately delete certain information where retaining it is reasonably necessary or required by law, for accounting or tax purposes, to fulfil a contract, to resolve a dispute, to prevent fraud or misuse, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

Where deletion cannot be completed because of a lawful retention requirement, the information will be retained only for as long as the relevant reason continues to apply, where reasonably practicable.

21. Secure Deletion

When information is no longer reasonably required and there is no lawful reason to retain it, WebForge Studio may delete or securely dispose of the information using reasonable measures appropriate to the type of information and storage system involved.

22. Electronic Information

Electronic information may be deleted from relevant systems, accounts, devices, databases, storage locations, or other controlled environments when it is no longer reasonably required.

Certain copies may remain temporarily in backups, archives, or system recovery environments and may be removed in accordance with the relevant system's normal retention cycle.

23. Physical Records

Where WebForge Studio holds physical records containing information that is no longer reasonably required, those records may be securely destroyed or disposed of using reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information.

24. Backups and Archived Information

Information may exist in technical backups, disaster-recovery systems, archives, or other protected storage after it has been removed from active systems.

Such information may remain until the relevant backup or archive is securely overwritten, deleted, or otherwise removed according to applicable retention practices.

25. Anonymisation

Where appropriate, WebForge Studio may anonymise information instead of deleting it, particularly where aggregated or non-identifiable information remains useful for legitimate business, operational, analytical, or statistical purposes.

Information that has been effectively anonymised will no longer be treated as personal information to the extent permitted by applicable law.

26. Data Security During Retention

WebForge Studio aims to apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect retained information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

The level of protection may depend on the nature and sensitivity of the information and the systems used to store or process it.

27. Third-Party Service Providers

WebForge Studio may use third-party providers for services such as hosting, cloud storage, email, payment processing, analytics, communications, backups, or other business functions.

Information stored or processed by such providers may be subject to their own systems, retention periods, and contractual or legal requirements.

28. Data Breaches and Security Incidents

Where WebForge Studio becomes aware of a security incident involving retained information, it may investigate the incident and take reasonable steps to contain, remediate, and prevent further unauthorised access or disclosure.

Where applicable, affected parties or relevant authorities may be notified in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

29. Client Project Data

Clients are responsible for identifying information within their projects that requires special retention, deletion, confidentiality, or handling requirements.

Where specific requirements are agreed in writing, WebForge Studio will consider those requirements subject to technical feasibility, contractual terms, applicable law, and legitimate legal obligations.

30. Policy Review

WebForge Studio may review and update this Data Retention & Deletion Policy from time to time to reflect changes in its services, technology, business operations, security practices, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version published on the WebForge Studio website will represent the current version of this policy.

31. Governing Law

This Data Retention & Deletion Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to any mandatory rights, protections, and remedies provided by applicable law.

32. Your Rights

Where applicable under South African law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including rights relating to access, correction, objection, or deletion, subject to lawful limitations and applicable requirements.

Requests concerning personal information will be considered in accordance with the WebForge Studio Privacy Policy and applicable privacy legislation.
