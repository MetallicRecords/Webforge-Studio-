1. Purpose of This Policy

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how WebForge Studio delivers digital services, digital products, website files, project deliverables, and any applicable physical items supplied as part of an agreed service.

Because WebForge Studio primarily provides digital services, most deliveries are made electronically or through online platforms rather than by physical shipment.

This policy is intended to provide clear expectations for both WebForge Studio and its clients while preserving any rights and remedies that apply under South African law.

2. Digital Service Delivery

Websites, website files, designs, documents, digital assets, and other agreed digital deliverables may be delivered electronically, uploaded to an agreed hosting environment, provided through an online platform, or made available using another reasonable digital delivery method.

The delivery method may depend on the type of project, the agreed scope, the client's technical requirements, and any third-party platform involved.

3. Delivery Commencement

WebForge Studio will generally begin the agreed project after the required project confirmation steps have been completed, including receipt of the required upfront payment and any information or materials reasonably necessary to commence the work.

Work may be delayed where required client information, content, approvals, access credentials, or other project requirements have not been provided.

4. Estimated Delivery Times

Any delivery timeframe provided by WebForge Studio is an estimate unless a specific delivery date has been expressly agreed in writing.

Estimated timelines may depend on project complexity, client responsiveness, requested changes, technical issues, third-party services, and other circumstances that may affect completion.

5. Client Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for providing accurate information, content, images, branding materials, approvals, access details, and other reasonable requirements needed for the agreed project.

Where a client does not provide required information or approvals within a reasonable period, the delivery timeline may be adjusted accordingly.

6. Website Delivery and Launch

Where a website is included in the agreed service, delivery may include publishing the completed website to the agreed hosting environment or providing the applicable website files to the client.

A website will be considered ready for delivery when the agreed scope has been completed and the applicable client approvals or information required for launch have been received.

7. Client Review and Approval

Where the project includes a review or approval stage, WebForge Studio may provide the client with an opportunity to review the relevant deliverable before final delivery or launch.

Clients are responsible for reviewing supplied information, content, spelling, contact details, prices, images, links, and other business information before approving a final deliverable.

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to correct errors attributable to WebForge Studio that are identified within the agreed scope.

8. Delivery Delays Caused by the Client

Delivery may be delayed where the client does not provide required content, information, approvals, feedback, account access, or other materials within a reasonable period.

Such delays may result in the estimated delivery date being moved without WebForge Studio being responsible for the resulting delay, subject to any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

9. Delays Outside Our Reasonable Control

WebForge Studio will not be responsible for delays caused by circumstances outside its reasonable control, including hosting outages, domain or DNS issues, third-party platform failures, internet disruptions, cyber incidents, power interruptions, natural events, or other events that could not reasonably have been prevented or anticipated.

Where such circumstances occur, WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to continue or resume the affected service as soon as reasonably practicable.

10. Third-Party Hosting and Platforms

Where delivery depends on a third-party hosting provider, domain registrar, software platform, payment provider, or other external service, delivery may be affected by the availability and requirements of that provider.

WebForge Studio cannot guarantee the uninterrupted availability or performance of third-party services that are outside its reasonable control.

11. Delivery of Project Files

Where the client is entitled to receive project files, WebForge Studio may provide those files electronically using a suitable delivery method.

The files supplied will depend on the agreed service and project scope. WebForge Studio is not required to provide internal tools, proprietary systems, confidential business information, or third-party materials that the client is not entitled to receive.

12. Website Hosting Delivery

Where website hosting is included in the agreed service, WebForge Studio may publish or configure the website on the applicable hosting environment once the requirements for launch have been completed.

Hosting availability, server performance, maintenance, outages, and other matters controlled by the hosting provider remain subject to that provider's terms and conditions.

13. Domain Registration and DNS

Where WebForge Studio assists with domain registration, DNS configuration, or related services, completion may depend on the relevant domain registrar and DNS provider.

Domain registration, renewal, ownership, transfer requirements, and related charges may be subject to the rules and terms of the applicable registrar.

Clients are responsible for providing accurate registration information and approving applicable domain-related decisions.

14. Physical Deliveries

WebForge Studio primarily provides digital services. If a physical item is specifically included in a client's agreed order, the applicable quotation or agreement will determine the delivery method, delivery location, and any applicable delivery charges.

Where a third-party courier or delivery service is used, delivery may also be subject to that provider's terms and operational limitations.

15. Delivery Confirmation

Digital delivery may be confirmed through an email, message, platform notification, website publication, file-transfer record, or another reasonable method showing that the relevant deliverable was made available.

A client is responsible for checking the delivery method and notifying WebForge Studio within a reasonable period if an agreed deliverable cannot be accessed or appears to be missing.

16. Client Access and Downloading

Where a digital deliverable is provided through a download link, online platform, cloud service, email, or another electronic method, the client is responsible for having suitable internet access and compatible equipment to receive or access the deliverable.

WebForge Studio may reasonably assist with access problems where the issue relates to the agreed service. Problems caused by the client's device, internet connection, email provider, storage limitations, or third-party platform may fall outside our control.

17. Incorrect Client Information

Clients are responsible for ensuring that delivery information, email addresses, domain details, hosting information, and other information supplied to WebForge Studio is accurate.

WebForge Studio will not be responsible for a delivery failure caused by incorrect or incomplete information supplied by the client, subject to applicable law.

18. Client Unavailability

Where client approval, feedback, access, or confirmation is required before delivery or launch, WebForge Studio may pause the affected stage until the client responds.

The project timeline may be adjusted to account for periods in which the required client response is unavailable.

19. Re-Delivery of Digital Files

Where a previously delivered digital file is reasonably required again, WebForge Studio may provide a replacement copy where the file remains available and the request relates to the agreed service.

WebForge Studio may not be able to reproduce or re-deliver third-party materials, discontinued services, or files that are no longer reasonably available.

20. Completion of Delivery

Delivery of a project does not necessarily mean that every future maintenance, update, hosting, support, or additional service is included.

Only the services and deliverables included in the applicable quotation, package, or written agreement form part of the original delivery obligation.

21. Delivery of Online Stores

Where an online store forms part of the agreed service, delivery may include the configuration and publication of the agreed store functionality, product listings, payment-related integrations, and other features specifically included in the quotation or package.

The availability of payment gateways, shipping integrations, domain services, hosting, and other third-party functionality may depend on the relevant external provider.

22. Product and Content Information

Clients are responsible for providing accurate product names, descriptions, prices, images, specifications, stock information, business information, and other content required for an online store or website.

WebForge Studio may rely on information supplied by the client when preparing or publishing the agreed deliverables.

23. Customer Orders and Physical Goods

Where a WebForge Studio project creates or supports an online store through which the client's own physical products are sold, the client's responsibility for fulfilling those customer orders remains separate from WebForge Studio's responsibility for providing the agreed website or digital service.

Unless expressly agreed in writing, WebForge Studio is not the seller, manufacturer, warehouse operator, or courier for products sold by a client through that client's online store.

24. Delivery After Client Approval

Where final approval is required, WebForge Studio may complete final delivery or publication after the client has provided the necessary approval.

If the client does not respond within a reasonable period after being notified that approval is required, WebForge Studio may place the project on hold until the required response is received.

25. Delivery Issues and Complaints

If a client experiences a genuine problem accessing an agreed deliverable, the client should notify WebForge Studio as soon as reasonably possible and provide sufficient information to identify the problem.

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to investigate and resolve delivery problems that fall within its responsibility.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude any mandatory right or remedy available to a client under applicable South African law.

26. Failed or Delayed Delivery

If delivery cannot be completed because of circumstances attributable to WebForge Studio, we will make reasonable efforts to correct the issue and complete the agreed delivery as soon as reasonably practicable.

Where the failure or delay results from the client, a third-party provider, technical infrastructure, or circumstances outside our reasonable control, the delivery timeline may be adjusted accordingly.

27. Loss or Corruption of Digital Files

WebForge Studio will take reasonable precautions when handling and delivering digital files. However, clients should maintain appropriate backups of important business information, content, and files supplied to or received from WebForge Studio.

Where a file is lost or corrupted due to circumstances within our reasonable control, WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to restore or re-deliver the affected agreed deliverable where it remains available.

28. Delivery and Intellectual Property

Delivery of a website, design, document, source file, or other digital deliverable does not by itself transfer ownership of every component used to create that deliverable.

Ownership and permitted use of the final deliverables will be determined by the applicable quotation, agreement, intellectual property terms, and applicable law.

Third-party software, licences, fonts, images, plugins, templates, APIs, and other licensed materials remain subject to their respective licence conditions.

29. Client Failure to Collect or Access a Delivery

Where a client fails to access, download, collect, or respond to a delivery notification within a reasonable period, WebForge Studio may retain the deliverable for a reasonable period where practicable.

Clients should notify WebForge Studio promptly if they experience a genuine access problem.

30. Policy Changes

WebForge Studio may update this Shipping & Delivery Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, delivery methods, technology, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

The version published on the WebForge Studio website will contain the applicable effective date and last-updated date.

31. Governing Law

This Shipping & Delivery Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to any mandatory rights and protections that apply by law.

32. No Unlawful Exclusion of Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict, or waive any right or remedy that WebForge Studio is not legally permitted to exclude or limit under applicable South African law.

Where applicable law provides a client with a greater protection or remedy than this policy, the applicable legal requirement will take precedence.

33. Fair Delivery Practices

WebForge Studio aims to provide agreed deliverables professionally, within reasonable timeframes, and in accordance with the applicable quotation, package, or written agreement.

Both WebForge Studio and its clients are expected to cooperate reasonably to facilitate successful delivery.
