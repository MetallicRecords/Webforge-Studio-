Payment Policy

Information about payments, invoices, deposits, and project-related charges.

Effective Date: [26 August 2026]
Last Updated: [26 August 2026]

1. Introduction

This Payment Policy explains the payment terms that apply to services purchased from WebForge Studio.

This policy should be read together with the WebForge Studio Terms & Conditions, Refund & Cancellation Policy, and any applicable quotation or agreement.

2. Service Pricing

WebForge Studio offers different service packages and digital solutions. Prices displayed on the website may represent starting prices and may vary depending on the customer's specific requirements.

The final price for a project will be confirmed before work begins where a quotation is required.

3. Deposits & Upfront Payments

WebForge Studio may require a deposit or upfront payment before beginning work on a project.

Work may only begin once the required payment has been received and confirmed.

4. Project Payments

Where a project is divided into payment stages, each payment must be made according to the agreed payment schedule.

Failure to make a required payment may result in work being paused until the outstanding amount has been received.

5. Additional Work

Work requested outside the original agreed scope may require an additional charge.

Where reasonably possible, WebForge Studio will inform the customer of additional costs before carrying out the additional work.

6. Third-Party Costs

Certain services may involve third-party costs, including domains, hosting, subscriptions, software, plugins, licences, payment services, or other external services.

Such costs may be charged separately where they are not included in the agreed project price.

7. Payment Methods

Available payment methods will be communicated by WebForge Studio during the quotation, ordering, or payment process.

Customers must provide accurate payment information and ensure that payments are successfully completed.

8. Payment Confirmation

A payment is considered received once the applicable payment provider or WebForge Studio confirms that the funds have been successfully received.

Customers should retain proof of payment where appropriate.

9. Failed or Reversed Payments

If a payment fails, is reversed, rejected, cancelled, or otherwise not successfully received, WebForge Studio may pause the relevant service or project until the outstanding amount has been resolved.

10. Overdue Payments

Where a payment becomes overdue, WebForge Studio may temporarily suspend work or access to applicable services until the outstanding payment has been made.

Any applicable consequences of late payment will be communicated to the customer where required.

11. Invoices

Where an invoice is issued, the customer is responsible for reviewing the invoice and making payment according to the stated terms.

Customers should contact WebForge Studio promptly if they believe an invoice contains an error.

12. Refunds

Payments and refunds are subject to the WebForge Studio Refund & Cancellation Policy and applicable law.

View Refund & Cancellation Policy

13. Taxes & Charges

Where applicable, taxes, transaction charges, payment processing fees, or other legally required charges may apply to a transaction.

14. Customer Responsibility

The customer is responsible for ensuring that payment information is accurate and that sufficient funds are available when payment is due.

15. Project Suspension

If required payments are not received, WebForge Studio may suspend project work until the outstanding amount has been resolved.

Project timelines may be adjusted as a result of payment-related delays.

16. Cancellation

Cancellation of a service or project does not automatically entitle the customer to a full refund.

Refund eligibility will be determined according to the applicable Refund & Cancellation Policy and applicable law.

17. Changes to This Policy

WebForge Studio may update this Payment Policy when necessary to reflect changes to services, payment methods, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

18. Governing Law

This policy is intended for WebForge Studio in South Africa and is subject to applicable South African law.
