1. Purpose of This Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy explains the types of activities and behaviour that are permitted and prohibited when using WebForge Studio's website, services, digital solutions, systems, and related resources.

The purpose of this policy is to help maintain a safe, lawful, reliable, respectful, and secure environment for WebForge Studio, its clients, visitors, service providers, and other users.

2. Lawful Use

Visitors and clients must use WebForge Studio's website and services only for lawful purposes and in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Users must not use WebForge Studio's services to facilitate, promote, support, or participate in unlawful activity.

3. Prohibited Activities

Users must not use WebForge Studio's website, services, systems, or digital resources for activities that may unlawfully harm WebForge Studio, another person, an organisation, a device, a network, or a third-party service.

Prohibited activities may include fraud, unauthorised access, malicious interference, distribution of harmful software, abuse of systems, or other unlawful conduct.

4. Fraud and Deception

Users must not use WebForge Studio's services to impersonate another person or organisation, provide intentionally misleading information, conduct fraudulent transactions, or otherwise deceive WebForge Studio, its clients, or other users.

5. Harmful or Malicious Activity

Users must not intentionally introduce viruses, malware, malicious code, harmful files, or other technology designed to damage, disrupt, compromise, or gain unauthorised access to systems or data.

Attempts to interfere with the normal operation, availability, integrity, or security of WebForge Studio systems are prohibited.

6. Unauthorised Access

Users must not attempt to gain unauthorised access to WebForge Studio systems, accounts, servers, databases, files, applications, networks, or other resources.

Users must not attempt to bypass authentication, security controls, access restrictions, or other technical safeguards.

7. Security Testing

Security testing, vulnerability testing, penetration testing, or similar activity involving WebForge Studio systems may only be performed with appropriate prior authorisation.

Unauthorised security testing or attempts to exploit vulnerabilities may be treated as prohibited activity.

8. Interference with Services

Users must not intentionally interfere with the availability, performance, operation, or security of WebForge Studio's website, services, systems, or digital infrastructure.

This includes deliberate attempts to overload, disrupt, disable, or impair systems or services.

9. Unauthorised Data Access

Users must not access, obtain, copy, modify, disclose, delete, or otherwise use data belonging to WebForge Studio, its clients, or other users without appropriate authorisation.

Access to information must remain within the permissions and purpose for which access was legitimately provided.

10. Spam and Unwanted Communications

WebForge Studio's website, contact forms, communication channels, or other services must not be used to send spam, bulk unsolicited communications, misleading messages, or other abusive communications.

Automated submissions or repeated communications designed to disrupt or abuse a service are also prohibited.

11. Abuse of Contact and Enquiry Services

Contact forms, enquiry forms, email addresses, and other communication channels provided by WebForge Studio must be used for genuine and appropriate communication.

Users must not use these channels to send threats, harassment, intentionally harmful material, fraudulent requests, or repeated messages intended to disrupt normal business operations.

12. Offensive or Abusive Content

Users must not knowingly submit or distribute content through WebForge Studio services that is unlawful, threatening, seriously abusive, malicious, or intended to cause harm.

WebForge Studio may take reasonable action where submitted material creates a legitimate legal, security, operational, or safety concern.

13. Intellectual Property

Users must respect the intellectual property rights of WebForge Studio, its clients, third-party providers, and other rights holders.

Users must not knowingly upload, copy, reproduce, distribute, publish, or use protected material without the appropriate rights, authorisation, or licence.

14. Privacy and Personal Information

Users must not use WebForge Studio services to unlawfully collect, disclose, misuse, or distribute another person's personal information.

Personal information submitted through WebForge Studio services must be handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws and the WebForge Studio Privacy Policy.

15. Misuse of Third-Party Services

Where WebForge Studio services connect with or rely on third-party platforms, users must comply with the applicable rules, terms, and acceptable-use requirements of those third parties.

Users must not use a WebForge Studio integration as a means of circumventing the restrictions or security controls of another service.

16. Account and Access Credentials

Where WebForge Studio provides account credentials, access details, administrative access, or other authentication information, users are responsible for keeping those credentials reasonably secure.

Users must not knowingly share access credentials with unauthorised persons or use another person's credentials without permission.

17. Unauthorised Use of Accounts

Users must promptly notify WebForge Studio if they become aware of suspected unauthorised access to an account, project, system, or service under their control.

Users must not access or attempt to access another user's account or resources without appropriate authorisation.

18. Prohibited Commercial Misuse

WebForge Studio services must not be used to conduct fraudulent schemes, deceptive commercial activity, unlawful transactions, or other business activities that violate applicable law.

Users must also comply with any applicable terms governing third-party platforms, payment providers, hosting services, software, or other external resources used in connection with a project.

19. Unauthorised Copying or Distribution

Users must not unlawfully copy, reproduce, redistribute, sell, or publish WebForge Studio materials, protected project materials, or third-party materials where the user does not have the necessary rights or permission.

Additional restrictions may apply under the WebForge Studio Intellectual Property Policy or the applicable project agreement.

20. Automated Access

Automated tools, scripts, bots, crawlers, or other systems must not be used to access WebForge Studio services in a manner that places unreasonable load on systems, bypasses restrictions, collects information without authorisation, or interferes with normal operation.

Legitimate automated access may be permitted where it is authorised and does not compromise the security, availability, or proper functioning of the relevant service.

21. Security and System Integrity

Users must not knowingly perform any activity that compromises or attempts to compromise the security, integrity, availability, or reliability of WebForge Studio systems or services.

This includes attempting to introduce malicious software, exploit vulnerabilities without authorisation, interfere with security controls, or gain access to restricted resources.

22. Harm to Other Users

Users must not use WebForge Studio services in a way that knowingly causes unlawful harm, harassment, intimidation, disruption, or significant interference to another user or third party.

Genuine complaints, disputes, criticism, and lawful communication are not prohibited merely because they may be negative or disagreeable.

23. Misuse of Website Content

Website content and resources made available by WebForge Studio must not be copied, extracted, reproduced, republished, or commercially exploited in violation of applicable intellectual property rights, licence terms, or other legal restrictions.

24. Client Project Misuse

Clients must not intentionally use a website, application, digital solution, hosting environment, or other service supplied by WebForge Studio for unlawful activity or activity that violates the agreed project requirements.

Clients are responsible for the lawful use of content, products, services, transactions, and information they publish through systems provided for their use.

25. Compliance with Applicable Policies

Users are expected to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy together with the WebForge Studio Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Intellectual Property Policy, Payment Policy, and other applicable policies.

Where a specific service has additional acceptable-use requirements, those requirements may also apply.

26. Enforcement

WebForge Studio may take reasonable and lawful action when it becomes aware of a suspected or confirmed violation of this Acceptable Use Policy.

Depending on the circumstances, action may include requesting that prohibited activity stop, restricting access, suspending an affected service, removing unlawful material where appropriate, or pursuing available legal remedies.

27. Suspension of Services

WebForge Studio may suspend or restrict a service where reasonably necessary to protect its systems, users, clients, intellectual property, data, or legitimate business interests.

Where appropriate and legally required, WebForge Studio will provide reasonable notice or an opportunity to address the issue before taking action.

28. Emergency Action

Where there is an immediate and credible risk involving security, fraud, unlawful activity, significant system disruption, or serious harm, WebForge Studio may take immediate reasonable measures to protect affected systems, users, clients, or third parties.

29. Reporting Violations

If you believe that WebForge Studio services are being used in violation of this policy, you may report the concern to WebForge Studio using the official contact details provided below.

Reports should contain sufficient information to allow the matter to be reasonably understood and investigated.

30. Policy Updates

WebForge Studio may update this Acceptable Use Policy from time to time to reflect changes to its services, technology, security practices, business operations, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version published on the WebForge Studio website will represent the current version of this policy.

31. Governing Law

This Acceptable Use Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to any mandatory rights, protections, and remedies provided by applicable law.

32. No Waiver of Legal Rights

Nothing in this Acceptable Use Policy is intended to exclude, restrict, or remove any right or protection that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable South African law.

33. Policy and Other Agreements

This Acceptable Use Policy should be read together with the WebForge Studio Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund & Cancellation Policy, Payment Policy, Intellectual Property Policy, Accessibility Policy, and other applicable policies.

Where a project or service has additional written requirements, those requirements may also apply.
