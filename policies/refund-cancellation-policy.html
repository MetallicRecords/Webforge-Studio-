1. Purpose of This Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how WebForge Studio handles cancellations, refund requests, project termination, and related matters for its digital services.

The policy is intended to provide reasonable protection for both WebForge Studio and its clients while recognising any mandatory rights and remedies available under applicable South African law.

2. Project Confirmation

A project may be considered confirmed once the client has accepted the applicable quotation, package, or written agreement and has completed the required steps for the project to commence.

WebForge Studio may reserve development time, resources, and third-party services for a confirmed project.

Clients should therefore ensure that they are ready to proceed before confirming a project.

3. Upfront Payment Before Work Begins

The agreed upfront payment or deposit is required before WebForge Studio begins project work, unless a different written arrangement has been agreed.

The upfront payment allows WebForge Studio to allocate time and resources to the project and begin the agreed work.

Where a client cancels after work has commenced, the amount already paid may be considered together with the work performed, resources committed, and applicable legal rights when assessing any refund.

4. Client Cancellation Before Work Begins

A client who wishes to cancel before work begins should notify WebForge Studio as soon as reasonably possible.

Any refund request will be assessed according to the applicable quotation or agreement, the circumstances of the cancellation, amounts already committed to third parties, and applicable South African consumer protection requirements.

Nothing in this policy is intended to unlawfully remove a consumer's statutory right to cancel or receive a remedy where such a right applies.

5. Cancellation After Work Has Started

If a client cancels a project after WebForge Studio has started performing the agreed services, WebForge Studio may take into account work already completed, time allocated, development already performed, approved deliverables, and reasonable non-refundable third-party costs.

Any refund or amount owing will be determined fairly and in accordance with the applicable agreement and mandatory requirements of South African law.

6. Refund Requests

A client requesting a refund should contact WebForge Studio and provide sufficient information for the request to be reviewed.

WebForge Studio will consider each refund request based on the relevant quotation or agreement, the services already performed, the stage of the project, amounts paid, third-party costs, and applicable legal requirements.

Where a refund is approved, the applicable refund amount and method will be communicated to the client.

7. Non-Refundable Third-Party Costs

Where WebForge Studio has incurred legitimate third-party costs on behalf of a client, such as domain registration, hosting, software licences, paid services, or other approved project expenses, those costs may be taken into account when assessing a refund.

WebForge Studio will not represent a third-party charge as refundable where the relevant provider has not made that charge refundable, subject to any rights the client may have under applicable law.

8. Delays Caused by the Client

A project may be delayed where the client does not provide required information, materials, approvals, feedback, account access, or other reasonable requirements needed to continue the work.

Such delays do not automatically create a right to a refund or cancellation without considering the circumstances and applicable legal requirements.

WebForge Studio may communicate with the client to establish a reasonable revised timeline where the project can still be completed.

9. Change of Mind

Where a client changes their mind after confirming a project or requesting work to begin, WebForge Studio will review the cancellation according to the applicable agreement, work already performed, costs incurred, and applicable South African law.

A change of mind does not automatically cancel obligations that have already arisen, subject always to any mandatory consumer rights.

10. Cancellation by WebForge Studio

WebForge Studio may cancel or suspend a service where reasonably necessary, including where there is serious misconduct, unlawful activity, fraud, abuse of the service, non-payment, or circumstances that make continued performance unreasonable or impossible.

Where WebForge Studio cancels a project for reasons within its control and a refund is legally or contractually due, the applicable refund will be assessed and handled fairly.

11. Refunds for Defective or Unsatisfactory Services

If a client believes that a service has not been provided in accordance with the agreed requirements, the client should contact WebForge Studio promptly and explain the issue.

WebForge Studio will first have a reasonable opportunity to investigate the concern and, where appropriate, correct or complete the affected work.

Where a refund, replacement, correction, or other remedy is required by applicable law, WebForge Studio will provide the applicable remedy in accordance with that law.

12. Website and Digital Product Corrections

Where an agreed deliverable contains an error attributable to WebForge Studio, the client should notify us within a reasonable period after discovering the issue.

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to correct errors that fall within the agreed project scope.

Changes resulting from new requirements, client-supplied information, third-party changes, or requests outside the original scope may be treated as additional work.

13. Approved Deliverables

Once a client has reviewed and approved a deliverable, subsequent changes requested because of a change in preference or direction may not automatically qualify for a refund.

WebForge Studio will nevertheless consider legitimate complaints and applicable consumer rights fairly and in accordance with South African law.

14. Refunds for Digital Services

Because digital services may involve work being performed, development time being allocated, digital files being created, or third-party resources being purchased, refund requests for digital services may depend on the stage and nature of the service.

WebForge Studio will assess such requests fairly and will not unlawfully exclude any refund or remedy that is required under applicable law.

15. Refund Processing

Where WebForge Studio approves a refund, the client may be asked to provide the information reasonably necessary to process the refund.

Refunds will normally be processed using a reasonable payment method appropriate to the original transaction, where practicable.

Processing times may depend on the payment method, financial institution, payment provider, or other circumstances outside the reasonable control of WebForge Studio.

16. Refund Exclusions and Limitations

A refund will not automatically be available where a client has received the agreed service or deliverable and the request is based solely on a change of preference, unless a refund or other remedy is required by applicable law.

WebForge Studio may also consider whether the issue complained of resulted from information, instructions, materials, approvals, or changes supplied by the client or from circumstances outside the reasonable control of WebForge Studio.

Nothing in this section is intended to exclude or limit any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under South African law.

17. Client-Requested Changes After Approval

Changes requested after approval of a design, website, digital product, or other deliverable may be treated as additional work where those changes fall outside the original agreed scope.

Additional work may require a new quotation or written approval before WebForge Studio proceeds.

A request for additional changes does not automatically create a right to cancel the original project or receive a refund for work already completed.

18. Abandoned Projects

A project may be considered inactive or abandoned where the client repeatedly fails to provide required information, approvals, feedback, access, or other reasonable requirements needed to continue the project.

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to contact the client before treating a project as abandoned.

Where a project is abandoned, WebForge Studio may assess the work already performed, resources committed, and applicable contractual and legal obligations before determining whether any refund or outstanding amount applies.

19. Failure to Make Required Payments

Where a required payment becomes overdue, WebForge Studio may pause work until the outstanding amount is resolved, subject to applicable law and the relevant agreement.

Delays caused by overdue payments may result in the project timeline being adjusted.

WebForge Studio may take reasonable steps to recover amounts lawfully due to the company where payment obligations remain outstanding.

20. Fraudulent or Abusive Refund Claims

WebForge Studio will consider refund requests fairly and in good faith. However, deliberately false, fraudulent, abusive, or misleading refund claims may be rejected where permitted by law.

WebForge Studio may retain relevant records and supporting information where reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, protect the business, or establish or defend legal rights.

21. Third-Party Services and Costs

Some projects may depend on third-party services such as hosting, domain registration, payment providers, software, plugins, APIs, or other external platforms.

Where a third-party service has already been purchased or activated for a client, its own cancellation, refund, and usage terms may apply.

WebForge Studio cannot guarantee a refund from a third-party provider where that provider's terms do not permit a refund. Any amounts recovered from such a provider will be handled in accordance with the applicable agreement and law.

22. Domain Names and Hosting

Domain names and hosting services may be subject to registration periods, renewal fees, provider rules, and other third-party conditions.

Where these services have been purchased on behalf of a client, cancellation or refund will depend on the relevant provider's terms and any applicable legal rights.

WebForge Studio is not responsible for losing a domain name, hosting account, or associated service where the client fails to provide required information or fails to approve or pay for a renewal when required.

23. Services Already Delivered

Where a service or digital deliverable has already been supplied, the availability of a refund will depend on the nature of the service, the agreed terms, the circumstances of the request, and applicable law.

WebForge Studio will not intentionally use this policy to prevent a client from exercising a lawful right to a remedy.

24. Partial Refunds

Where appropriate, a refund may be calculated on a partial basis rather than as a full refund.

Factors that may be considered include the proportion of work completed, deliverables already supplied, approved work, third-party costs, and other legitimate project expenses.

Any applicable statutory consumer rights will take precedence over this provision.

25. Client Cooperation

Clients are expected to cooperate reasonably with WebForge Studio during cancellation or refund processes by providing accurate information and responding to reasonable requests.

Where additional information is reasonably required to assess a refund or cancellation, WebForge Studio may delay its assessment until the necessary information has been received.

26. Cancellation of Ongoing Services

Where a client receives an ongoing service from WebForge Studio, including maintenance, support, hosting assistance, or another recurring service, the client may request cancellation in accordance with the applicable agreement or service terms.

Cancellation of an ongoing service does not automatically cancel amounts that became due before the effective cancellation date.

Where a service is subject to a minimum agreed period, cancellation will be handled in accordance with that agreement and applicable South African law.

27. Effect of Cancellation

Following cancellation, WebForge Studio may stop work on the affected project or service after the cancellation has been processed.

The client may be required to settle amounts lawfully due for work completed or services supplied up to the effective cancellation date.

Where applicable, WebForge Studio will provide reasonable information concerning the status of the project and any deliverables that the client is entitled to receive.

28. Transfer or Handover After Cancellation

Where a client is entitled to receive project files, website information, credentials, or other deliverables following cancellation, WebForge Studio will provide the applicable items subject to the agreed scope, payment obligations, third-party restrictions, and applicable law.

Third-party accounts, licences, subscriptions, domains, hosting services, or other resources may remain subject to the terms of the relevant provider.

29. Disputes Concerning Refunds

If a client disagrees with a refund decision, the client should contact WebForge Studio and provide the relevant details so that the matter can be reviewed.

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to resolve legitimate concerns professionally and fairly.

Nothing in this policy prevents either party from exercising a lawful right to seek assistance, dispute resolution, or another remedy available under South African law.

30. Protection Against Unlawful or Abusive Conduct

WebForge Studio reserves the right to take reasonable and lawful measures to protect its business, staff, contractors, systems, intellectual property, and clients against fraud, harassment, threats, deliberate misuse, or other unlawful conduct.

This provision does not prevent a genuine client from exercising lawful consumer rights or raising a legitimate complaint.

31. Governing Law

This Refund & Cancellation Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to any mandatory consumer rights, protections, and remedies that apply by law.

32. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict, or waive a right or remedy that WebForge Studio is not legally permitted to exclude or limit under applicable South African law.

Where a mandatory legal requirement provides a client with a greater right or remedy than this policy, the applicable legal requirement will take precedence.

33. Fair and Reasonable Assessment

WebForge Studio aims to assess cancellation and refund requests consistently, reasonably, and in good faith.

Each request may be considered according to the individual circumstances, the applicable agreement, work already performed, services already supplied, legitimate costs incurred, and the rights and obligations of both parties.
