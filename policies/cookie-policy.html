1. Purpose of This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how WebForge Studio may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit or interact with our website.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar technologies that may be stored on a device when a website is accessed.

They can allow a website or service to recognise a device, remember certain settings, maintain functionality, improve security, or understand how the website is being used.

3. Why WebForge Studio May Use Cookies

WebForge Studio may use cookies or similar technologies for legitimate purposes such as website functionality, security, performance, visitor preferences, analytics, and improving the user experience.

Not every category will necessarily be used on the WebForge Studio website at all times.

5. Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies may be used where they are required for the website or a requested feature to operate, maintain security, prevent abuse, or provide a service requested by the visitor.

Because these cookies may be necessary for core functionality, some website features may not operate correctly if they are disabled.

6. Functionality Cookies

Functionality cookies may allow the website to remember information or preferences that improve the visitor's experience.

7. Analytics and Performance Cookies
