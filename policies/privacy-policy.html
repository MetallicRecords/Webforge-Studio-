1. Introduction

WebForge Studio respects the privacy of its clients, website visitors, prospective clients, and other individuals whose personal information may be provided to or processed by WebForge Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how WebForge Studio may collect, use, store, protect, and otherwise handle personal information in connection with its website, enquiries, quotations, products, services, and business operations.

WebForge Studio aims to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable South African privacy and data protection requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), where applicable.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with WebForge Studio, we may collect personal or business information that you voluntarily provide to us.

This may include your name, business name, email address, telephone number, physical or business address, website information, project requirements, quotation information, and other information necessary to provide our services.

Where you use an online store, enquiry form, communication channel, or other digital service, additional information may be collected where reasonably necessary for that service.

3. Information Collected Through Communication

When you contact WebForge Studio by email, telephone, website form, social media, or another communication channel, we may retain information necessary to respond to your enquiry and manage the related business communication.

We may use this information to provide quotations, discuss project requirements, provide customer support, maintain business records, and communicate with you regarding requested services.

4. Information From Website Forms

Information submitted through forms on the WebForge Studio website may be collected for the purpose stated on the relevant form.

Visitors should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through general enquiry forms.

5. How We Use Personal Information

WebForge Studio may use personal information for legitimate business purposes, including responding to enquiries, preparing quotations, delivering services, managing projects, providing customer support, and communicating with clients.

Personal information may also be used to maintain appropriate business records, administer agreements, process necessary transactions, protect our systems and business, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Lawful Processing

WebForge Studio aims to process personal information only where there is an appropriate lawful basis for doing so under applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include consent, the performance of a contract or steps requested before entering into a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of legitimate interests, or another lawful basis recognised by applicable South African law.

Where consent is relied upon, individuals may withdraw that consent where the law permits them to do so. Withdrawal of consent does not necessarily affect processing that was lawfully carried out before the withdrawal.

7. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

WebForge Studio does not intentionally request sensitive personal information through ordinary website enquiries unless it is reasonably necessary for a specific service and there is an appropriate lawful basis for processing it.

If sensitive information is submitted unnecessarily, WebForge Studio may take reasonable steps to avoid retaining or using that information where it is not required.

8. Sharing Personal Information

WebForge Studio does not sell personal information to third parties.

Personal information may be shared with service providers or other authorised parties where reasonably necessary to provide an agreed service, operate our business, maintain digital systems, process transactions, or comply with legal requirements.

Where appropriate, WebForge Studio will take reasonable steps to ensure that parties handling personal information on its behalf provide appropriate protection and use the information only for authorised purposes.

9. Service Providers and Third Parties

WebForge Studio may use trusted third-party providers to support its business operations and deliver services to clients.

These providers may include hosting companies, domain registrars, website platforms, software providers, analytics services, payment service providers, communication platforms, and other technology providers.

Where personal information is processed by a third party, WebForge Studio will take reasonable steps to ensure that the processing is appropriate for the purpose for which the information was provided and that reasonable safeguards are applied where required by law.

10. Website Hosting and Technical Information

When visitors access the WebForge Studio website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the website, hosting provider, security systems, or other technical services.

This information may include technical identifiers, browser or device information, approximate usage information, security logs, and other information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the website.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

WebForge Studio may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to operate the website, improve functionality, understand website usage, or support legitimate business and security purposes.

Where applicable, visitors may be able to manage certain cookie preferences through their browser or available website controls.

Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

12. Direct Marketing

WebForge Studio may communicate with existing or prospective clients regarding services, quotations, projects, updates, or other relevant business information, subject to applicable law.

Where consent is required for direct marketing, WebForge Studio will seek consent in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Individuals may request that their personal information not be used for direct marketing where applicable.

13. Data Quality and Accuracy

WebForge Studio aims to keep personal information reasonably accurate, relevant, and up to date for the purposes for which it is processed.

Clients and other individuals are encouraged to notify WebForge Studio when their personal information changes or when they believe information held by us is inaccurate.

14. Retention of Personal Information

WebForge Studio will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business requirements, contractual obligations, or applicable legal requirements.

The length of time information is retained may depend on the type of information, the nature of the service provided, ongoing business relationships, and legal or regulatory requirements.

15. Security Safeguards

WebForge Studio takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised access, unauthorised disclosure, misuse, or other unlawful processing.

Security measures may include access controls, reasonable account protection, secure handling procedures, system monitoring, and other safeguards appropriate to the information and circumstances.

While reasonable precautions are taken, no electronic transmission, website, hosting environment, or digital storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

16. Data Breaches and Security Incidents

If WebForge Studio becomes aware of a security compromise involving personal information, we will take reasonable steps to assess and respond to the incident.

Where notification is required by applicable South African law, WebForge Studio will follow the applicable notification requirements and take reasonable steps to reduce potential harm.

17. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, individuals may have rights relating to personal information held by WebForge Studio.

These may include requesting access to personal information, asking for inaccurate information to be corrected, requesting appropriate deletion where legally applicable, objecting to certain processing, or raising concerns about the handling of personal information.

Requests will be considered in accordance with applicable South African privacy and data-protection requirements.

18. Requests for Access or Correction

Individuals who wish to request access to, correction of, or other lawful action concerning their personal information may contact WebForge Studio using the contact details provided in this policy.

WebForge Studio may need to verify the identity of the person making a request before releasing or changing personal information.

19. Children's Personal Information

WebForge Studio's services and website are primarily intended for businesses, clients, and general website visitors. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children where such collection is not permitted by applicable law.

If a parent, guardian, or other authorised person believes that personal information belonging to a child has been provided to WebForge Studio without appropriate authority, they may contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be considered.

20. Information Belonging to Businesses and Organisations

Where clients provide information about employees, customers, suppliers, contractors, or other individuals in connection with a project, the client is responsible for ensuring that they are authorised to provide that information and that the applicable privacy requirements have been considered.

WebForge Studio will handle such information only for legitimate purposes connected with the agreed services or other lawful business requirements.

21. International Transfers and External Platforms

Some third-party technology or service providers used in connection with WebForge Studio services may store or process information outside South Africa.

Where personal information is transferred or processed outside South Africa, WebForge Studio will take reasonable steps to comply with applicable legal requirements governing such transfers.

22. Links to Other Websites

The WebForge Studio website may contain links to external websites, platforms, or services.

WebForge Studio does not control the privacy practices of external websites and is not responsible for their privacy policies, security, content, or handling of personal information.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information to them.

23. Client Accounts and Access Credentials

Where WebForge Studio requires access to a client's hosting account, domain account, website, social media account, software platform, or another digital service, such access will be used only as reasonably necessary to perform the agreed service.

Clients should not provide unnecessary passwords or confidential credentials. Where possible, appropriate user permissions or temporary access should be used.

Clients remain responsible for securing their own accounts and changing credentials when access is no longer required.

24. Payment and Transaction Information

Where payment or transaction information is required in connection with a WebForge Studio service, the information will be processed only for legitimate business, transaction, accounting, security, or legal purposes.

WebForge Studio may retain certain personal information where reasonably necessary to maintain business records, establish or defend legal rights, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or misuse, or meet applicable accounting and regulatory requirements.

Information retained for these purposes will continue to be handled with reasonable care and in accordance with applicable law.

26. Protecting Our Business and Our Clients

WebForge Studio may process information where reasonably necessary to protect its business, clients, systems, intellectual property, staff, contractors, service providers, and other legitimate interests.

This may include detecting or preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, security threats, unlawful activity, misuse of services, or attempts to compromise WebForge Studio systems.

Such processing will be carried out subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate privacy rights.

27. Complaints and Privacy Concerns

If you believe that WebForge Studio has handled your personal information incorrectly or contrary to applicable privacy requirements, we encourage you to contact us first so that we can investigate and attempt to resolve the concern.
