Refund & Cancellation Policy

Our approach to payments, cancellations, refunds, and project changes.

Effective Date: [26 August 2026]
Last Updated: [26 August 2026]

1. Introduction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the conditions that apply when a customer purchases services from WebForge Studio and later requests a cancellation or refund.

This policy should be read together with the WebForge Studio Terms & Conditions.

2. Deposits & Upfront Payments

WebForge Studio may require a deposit or upfront payment before work begins.

Once work has started, the amount already used for work completed, preparation, design, development, research, configuration, or other project activities may not be refundable, subject to applicable law.

3. Customer Cancellation Before Work Begins

If a customer requests cancellation before work has started, WebForge Studio will assess the request and any refund that may be applicable.

Any costs already incurred specifically for the project, including third-party costs or non-refundable services, may be deducted where legally permitted.

4. Cancellation After Work Has Started

If a customer cancels a project after work has started, the customer may remain responsible for payment for work already completed and costs already incurred.

Where applicable, any remaining refundable amount will be determined after deducting amounts relating to work completed and approved project expenses.

5. Completed Work

Once a website, online store, digital solution, or other service has been substantially completed and approved by the customer, refunds may not be available for completed work, subject to applicable law.

6. Customer-Requested Changes

Changes that fall outside the original agreed project scope may require additional payment.

A customer cannot request a refund solely because they later decide they want a different design, additional features, or requirements that were not included in the original agreement.

7. Third-Party Costs

Costs paid to third-party providers may be non-refundable where the third party does not provide a refund.

This may include domain registration, hosting, subscriptions, software licences, plugins, paid services, payment-provider fees, or other third-party expenses.

8. Recurring Services

Where a customer purchases a recurring service, cancellation will generally prevent future billing after the applicable cancellation period.

Cancellation of a recurring service does not automatically create a refund for services already provided.

9. Refund Requests

Refund requests should be submitted to WebForge Studio using the official contact details.

A refund request should include the customer's name, relevant service or project, payment information, and reason for the request.

WebForge Studio may request additional information where reasonably necessary to assess the request.

10. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, WebForge Studio will process the refund using the applicable payment method or another reasonable method agreed with the customer.

The time required for the funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

11. Defective or Incorrect Services

If a customer believes that a service has not been delivered according to the agreed scope, the customer should contact WebForge Studio as soon as reasonably possible.

WebForge Studio will review the issue and, where appropriate, take reasonable steps to correct the relevant issue.

12. Customer Delays

Delays caused by the customer, including delays in providing content, information, approvals, payments, access credentials, or other required materials, do not automatically qualify the customer for a refund.

13. Abandoned Projects

If a customer stops communicating or fails to provide information required to continue a project for an extended period, WebForge Studio may treat the project as inactive or abandoned.

Amounts relating to work already completed may remain payable.

14. Online Store Services

For online-store projects, refunds are assessed based on the stage of development, work completed, third-party costs, and the specific agreement with the customer.

15. Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or limit any consumer rights or other legal rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

16. Changes to This Policy

WebForge Studio may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy when necessary.

The latest version will be published on this website with the applicable effective or revision date.

17. Governing Law

This policy is intended for WebForge Studio in South Africa and is subject to applicable South African law.
