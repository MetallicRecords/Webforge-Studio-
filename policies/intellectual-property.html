1. Purpose of This Policy

This Intellectual Property Policy explains the ownership, permitted use, protection, licensing, and handling of intellectual property associated with WebForge Studio, its clients, projects, and third-party materials.

The purpose of this policy is to protect WebForge Studio's legitimate intellectual property interests while respecting the lawful rights and ownership interests of clients and third parties.

2. What Is Intellectual Property?

Intellectual property may include original creative, technical, written, visual, digital, branding, software, design, or other protected works and materials.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, intellectual property may include copyright, trademarks, trade names, designs, software code, graphics, photographs, written content, databases, concepts, documentation, and other proprietary materials.

3. WebForge Studio Intellectual Property

Unless expressly transferred or licensed to a client in writing, intellectual property created, owned, or developed by WebForge Studio remains the property of WebForge Studio or its relevant licensors.

This may include reusable code, development methods, frameworks, templates, design systems, processes, tools, documentation, business materials, and other underlying resources developed independently by WebForge Studio.

4. Client-Owned Materials

A client generally retains ownership of intellectual property that the client lawfully owns and supplies to WebForge Studio for use in a project.

Such materials may include the client's business name, logo, photographs, written content, product information, trademarks, and other materials supplied by the client.

5. Client Permission to Use Materials

By supplying content or other materials to WebForge Studio, the client represents that the client has the necessary rights, permissions, or lawful authority to provide those materials for the agreed project.

The client grants WebForge Studio the permission reasonably necessary to use, reproduce, modify, display, store, and incorporate those materials for the purpose of providing the agreed services.

6. Project Deliverables

The intellectual property rights applicable to a final project deliverable will depend on the nature of the deliverable, the quotation or agreement, any applicable licence terms, and South African law.

Payment for a project does not, by itself, transfer every intellectual property right in every component used to create the project.

7. Transfer of Rights

Where WebForge Studio expressly agrees in writing to transfer particular intellectual property rights to a client, the transfer will apply only to the rights and materials identified in that written agreement.

Any rights not expressly transferred remain with their respective owner, subject to applicable law.

8. Licence to Client

Where ownership is not transferred but the client is permitted to use a WebForge Studio deliverable, the client receives only the licence or permission expressly granted by the applicable agreement.

The client may not assume that delivery of a file or website grants unrestricted rights to reproduce, resell, redistribute, modify, or commercially exploit underlying intellectual property.

9. Reusable Code and Components

WebForge Studio may use reusable code, libraries, development techniques, frameworks, components, structures, templates, or other resources when creating projects.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, the use of such resources does not transfer WebForge Studio's underlying rights in reusable materials to the client.

10. Third-Party Intellectual Property

Projects may contain or depend on intellectual property belonging to third parties, including software libraries, fonts, images, plugins, APIs, templates, hosting technology, and other licensed resources.

Such materials remain subject to the applicable third-party ownership, licence, usage restrictions, and other legal conditions.

11. Copyright

Copyright may apply to original literary, artistic, software, design, website, graphic, photographic, audiovisual, and other qualifying works created or supplied in connection with a project.

The ownership and permitted use of copyright-protected material will be determined by the applicable agreement, the nature of the material, and applicable South African copyright law.

12. Website Source Code

Website source code may consist of client-specific work, WebForge Studio's pre-existing or reusable code, third-party code, open-source components, or a combination of these.

Unless a written agreement expressly provides otherwise, providing access to website files does not automatically transfer ownership of WebForge Studio's pre-existing, reusable, proprietary, or third-party code.

13. Designs and Visual Assets

Website layouts, graphics, user-interface elements, illustrations, visual systems, and other creative assets may be protected by intellectual property rights.

The rights granted to a client will depend on the agreed scope, applicable licence terms, and any written intellectual property agreement.

14. Client Trademarks and Branding

Clients remain responsible for ensuring that logos, trade names, trademarks, slogans, photographs, and other branded materials they provide may lawfully be used in the project.

WebForge Studio does not claim ownership of a client's independently owned trademark or branding merely because those materials are used while providing the agreed services.

15. Portfolio and Promotional Use

Subject to the client's agreement and applicable confidentiality, privacy, intellectual property, and legal requirements, WebForge Studio may wish to display completed work as part of its portfolio or professional marketing materials.

Where a project contains confidential information or where a client has expressly agreed to reasonable restrictions on promotional use, WebForge Studio will take those restrictions into account.

16. Open-Source Software

WebForge Studio may use open-source software and other publicly licensed resources when developing websites, applications, or digital solutions.

Open-source materials remain subject to their respective licences. Where applicable, clients must comply with the conditions of those licences when using, modifying, distributing, or commercially exploiting the relevant material.

17. Fonts, Images, Icons, and Other Licensed Assets

Projects may use fonts, stock images, icons, graphics, plugins, or other assets obtained under licences from third-party providers.

Unless expressly transferred by their owner, these assets do not become the property of the client merely because they are included in a completed project.

Continued use of a third-party asset may be subject to the relevant licence, subscription, attribution, or usage restrictions.

18. Restrictions on Copying and Resale

No person may copy, reproduce, redistribute, resell, publish, or commercially exploit WebForge Studio's proprietary intellectual property without the appropriate permission or licence.

This includes proprietary code, original designs, documentation, templates, business materials, and other protected resources owned by WebForge Studio.

19. Unauthorised Modification

Where a project contains proprietary or licensed components, unauthorised modification, removal of ownership notices, or redistribution may be restricted by the applicable intellectual property rights or licence terms.

Clients should contact WebForge Studio if they require additional rights or permissions beyond those already granted.

20. Confidential and Proprietary Information

Certain information exchanged during a project may be confidential or commercially sensitive.

Clients and WebForge Studio should not knowingly disclose or misuse confidential proprietary information belonging to the other party, except where disclosure is authorised, required by law, or reasonably necessary to perform the agreed services.

21. Ownership of WebForge Studio Materials

WebForge Studio retains its rights in original materials that it develops independently and that are not expressly assigned to a client.

This may include internal tools, development methods, reusable components, templates, systems, processes, concepts, documentation, and other business or technical resources.

22. Pre-Existing Intellectual Property

Intellectual property that existed before a project began remains owned by its original owner unless a separate written agreement provides otherwise.

A client does not acquire ownership of WebForge Studio's pre-existing intellectual property merely because it is incorporated into or used to create a client project.

23. Project Files and Working Materials

Project files, drafts, working files, development notes, internal documentation, source materials, reusable components, and other working materials may not form part of the final deliverables unless they are expressly included in the applicable agreement.

WebForge Studio may retain copies of appropriate project materials for legitimate business, technical, security, record-keeping, or legal purposes, subject to applicable confidentiality and privacy requirements.

24. Intellectual Property Created by Contractors or Third Parties

WebForge Studio may work with contractors, freelancers, developers, designers, software providers, or other third parties in delivering certain projects.

Rights in materials created or supplied by such parties will be handled according to the relevant contracts, licences, assignments, and applicable law.

25. Infringement Claims

If a client or other person believes that material used on a WebForge Studio project or website infringes their intellectual property rights, they should notify WebForge Studio promptly with sufficient information to identify the material and explain the concern.

WebForge Studio may investigate genuine claims and, where appropriate, take reasonable steps to address confirmed infringement.

26. Reporting Suspected Infringement

Anyone who believes that material associated with WebForge Studio infringes their intellectual property rights should contact WebForge Studio and provide reasonable information supporting the claim.

Where appropriate, WebForge Studio may review the reported material and take reasonable steps to investigate and resolve the matter.

27. Removal or Replacement of Disputed Material

Where there is a credible intellectual property concern, WebForge Studio may temporarily remove, disable, replace, or restrict access to the relevant material while the matter is being investigated.

Any action taken will be considered in light of the applicable agreement, the rights of the relevant parties, and South African law.

28. Client Responsibility for Legal Rights

Clients are responsible for ensuring that materials they request WebForge Studio to publish, reproduce, modify, or incorporate into a project are lawful and do not knowingly infringe the rights of another person or organisation.

This includes materials such as text, photographs, logos, artwork, videos, music, trademarks, software, documents, and other protected content.

29. No Unauthorised Transfer of WebForge Studio Property

WebForge Studio's intellectual property may not be sold, transferred, sublicensed, copied, or distributed by a client or third party unless the applicable rights have been expressly granted or transferred in writing.

Any permitted use remains subject to the scope and conditions of the applicable licence or written agreement.

30. Protection of Intellectual Property Rights

WebForge Studio reserves the right to take reasonable and lawful measures to protect its intellectual property and the intellectual property entrusted to it by clients or third-party licensors.

Such measures may include restricting unauthorised copying, requesting removal of infringing material, suspending access where reasonably necessary, or pursuing available legal remedies.

31. Intellectual Property and Payment

Payment for a WebForge Studio service does not automatically transfer all intellectual property rights associated with the project.

Any ownership transfer, assignment, or licence granted to a client will be determined by the applicable quotation, written agreement, licence terms, and applicable law.

32. Intellectual Property and Project Completion

Completion or delivery of a project does not by itself transfer ownership of WebForge Studio's pre-existing materials, reusable systems, proprietary processes, third-party resources, or other intellectual property that has not expressly been assigned.

The client's rights to use the completed deliverables will remain subject to the applicable agreement and licence conditions.

33. Governing Law

This Intellectual Property Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to any mandatory rights, protections, and remedies provided by applicable law.
