1. Payment Requirements

WebForge Studio requires the applicable payment specified in the quotation, package, invoice, or written agreement before the relevant stage of a project or service is commenced.

Unless a different arrangement has been expressly agreed in writing, a client is required to pay a 50% upfront deposit before WebForge Studio begins work on a new project.

2. Upfront Deposit

The upfront deposit confirms the client's intention to proceed and allows WebForge Studio to allocate resources and commence the agreed work.

The deposit will be treated in accordance with the applicable quotation, agreement, cancellation terms, refund policy, and applicable South African law.

3. Balance of Payment

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the remaining balance becomes payable according to the payment schedule stated in the applicable quotation, invoice, or agreement.

WebForge Studio may delay final delivery, publication, transfer, or completion of services where an amount that is lawfully due remains unpaid, subject to applicable law.

4. Accepted Payment Methods

WebForge Studio will communicate the available payment method or payment details to the client through the applicable quotation, invoice, written communication, or other authorised business channel.

Clients should use only payment details officially supplied or confirmed by WebForge Studio.

5. Proof of Payment

WebForge Studio may request reasonable proof of payment where payment has been made but has not yet been reflected in the applicable account or payment records.

A payment will be treated as received once it has been successfully confirmed by WebForge Studio or the relevant payment provider, subject to applicable processing times.

6. Project Commencement

Work will generally commence after the required upfront payment has been received and the information, content, access credentials, approvals, and other materials reasonably required to begin the project have been provided.

WebForge Studio is not required to commence work where the required payment or essential project information has not been received.

7. Payment Deadlines

Clients are responsible for making payments by the deadlines stated in the applicable quotation, invoice, or written agreement.

Where no specific deadline is stated, payment is due within the period communicated by WebForge Studio or otherwise required by the applicable agreement and law.

8. Late Payment

Where an amount is lawfully due and remains unpaid after the applicable payment deadline, WebForge Studio may provide reasonable notice requesting payment.

Subject to the applicable agreement and South African law, WebForge Studio may suspend affected services or place a project on hold until the outstanding amount is resolved.

9. Project Suspension for Non-Payment

Where a client fails to make a required payment, WebForge Studio may temporarily suspend work on the affected project after giving any notice required by law or the applicable agreement.

Any resulting change to the project timeline may be taken into account when work resumes.

10. Additional Work

Work outside the agreed scope may require an additional quotation, fee, or written approval before the additional work is performed.

WebForge Studio will not intentionally treat an unrequested additional service as part of the original agreed price without appropriate communication and agreement.

11. Changes to Project Scope

Material changes to the agreed project scope may affect the total price, delivery timeframe, or payment schedule.

Where a scope change results in additional charges, WebForge Studio may provide the client with the applicable additional cost before proceeding with the changed work.

12. Third-Party Costs

Certain projects may require third-party services such as domain registration, hosting, software licences, plugins, subscriptions, payment gateways, or other external services.

Where such costs are the client's responsibility, they may be charged separately or included in the applicable quotation as communicated to the client.

13. Payment Processing Fees

Where a payment provider or financial institution applies a transaction, processing, currency conversion, or other legitimate fee, responsibility for that fee will depend on the applicable quotation, agreement, payment method, and applicable law.

14. Invoices and Records

WebForge Studio may issue quotations, invoices, receipts, payment confirmations, or other reasonable payment records for services provided.

Clients should retain their payment records and notify WebForge Studio promptly if they believe a payment record contains an error.

15. Payment Disputes

If a client believes that an invoice, payment amount, or payment record is incorrect, the client should contact WebForge Studio as soon as reasonably possible and provide the relevant details.

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to investigate genuine payment disputes and correct confirmed errors where appropriate.

16. Incorrect Payments

If a client accidentally makes an incorrect payment, including an overpayment or payment to an incorrect WebForge Studio payment destination, the client should notify WebForge Studio as soon as reasonably possible.

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to investigate and resolve confirmed payment errors in accordance with the applicable agreement and South African law.

17. Unauthorised or Fraudulent Payments

WebForge Studio may investigate payments that appear to be unauthorised, fraudulent, suspicious, reversed, or otherwise irregular.

Where reasonably necessary, WebForge Studio may temporarily suspend the affected project, service, account, delivery, or transaction while the matter is investigated.

18. Reversed or Failed Payments

If a payment is reversed, cancelled, rejected, charged back, or otherwise fails after being treated as received, WebForge Studio may request resolution of the outstanding amount before continuing the affected service, subject to applicable law.

Any legitimate payment-provider investigation or dispute process may also apply.

19. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Clients should contact WebForge Studio directly where they have a genuine concern regarding a payment, invoice, or service charge so that the matter can be investigated and resolved where possible.

WebForge Studio reserves the right to provide relevant transaction, invoice, communication, and service records to a payment provider, financial institution, or other authorised party where reasonably necessary to respond to a disputed transaction or protect the business against fraud.

20. No Payment for Unauthorised Additional Work

WebForge Studio will communicate material additional charges before carrying out additional work where reasonably practicable.

Clients are not expected to pay for work that was neither agreed nor reasonably authorised, except where the applicable agreement or law provides otherwise.

21. Payment and Project Ownership

Payment for a service does not automatically transfer ownership of every component, source file, licence, third-party asset, software component, or intellectual property used in producing the project.

Ownership and permitted use of final deliverables will be determined by the applicable quotation, agreement, intellectual property terms, and applicable South African law.

22. Final Delivery and Outstanding Amounts

Where a final balance is lawfully due before final delivery, WebForge Studio may withhold final delivery or publication until the required payment has been received and confirmed, subject to the client's statutory rights.

This provision does not remove any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

23. Hosting, Domains, and Subscriptions

Recurring services such as hosting, domain registration, software subscriptions, plugins, licences, maintenance plans, or other continuing services may require separate or recurring payments as stated in the applicable agreement.

Failure to maintain a required recurring payment may result in the relevant third-party service expiring, being suspended, or becoming unavailable.

24. Taxes and Statutory Charges

Prices, quotations, invoices, and charges will be presented in accordance with WebForge Studio's applicable pricing practices and any relevant tax or statutory requirements.

Where a legally applicable tax, levy, or statutory charge is required, it will be handled in accordance with the applicable law.

25. Client Cooperation

Clients are expected to cooperate reasonably with WebForge Studio in relation to payments, invoices, approvals, project information, and other matters necessary for the agreed service.

Unreasonable delays, abusive conduct, fraudulent payment activity, or repeated failure to comply with agreed payment obligations may result in the affected service being placed on hold or otherwise handled in accordance with the agreement and applicable law.

26. Suspension for Outstanding Payments

Where an amount is lawfully due and remains unpaid, WebForge Studio may, after providing any notice required by the applicable agreement or law, suspend the affected services or place the project on hold until the payment matter has been resolved.

Any suspension will be handled reasonably and subject to the rights and protections available to the client under applicable South African law.

27. Cancellation and Refunds

Cancellation of a service and any applicable refund will be handled according to the WebForge Studio Refund & Cancellation Policy, the applicable quotation or agreement, and mandatory requirements of South African law.

Clients should review the Refund & Cancellation Policy before requesting cancellation or a refund.

28. Payment Information and Privacy

Payment-related personal information will be handled in accordance with WebForge Studio's Privacy Policy and applicable privacy legislation, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) where applicable.

WebForge Studio will take reasonable measures to protect payment information within its control.

29. Protection Against Abuse and Fraud

WebForge Studio may take reasonable and lawful steps to protect the business against fraudulent transactions, dishonest payment activity, abuse of payment processes, deliberately misleading information, or other conduct that may cause financial or operational harm.

Such measures will not prevent a client from exercising a genuine legal right or raising a legitimate payment dispute.

30. Policy Updates

WebForge Studio may update this Payment Policy from time to time to reflect changes to its payment procedures, services, technology, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

The effective date and last-updated date shown in this policy will identify the current version published by WebForge Studio.

31. Governing Law

This Payment Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to any mandatory rights, protections, and remedies that apply under applicable law.

32. Fair and Lawful Payment Practices

WebForge Studio aims to administer payments fairly, transparently, and consistently while protecting the legitimate financial and operational interests of the business.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or unlawfully restrict any statutory right available to a client or consumer.

33. Payment Policy and Other Policies

This Payment Policy should be read together with the WebForge Studio Terms & Conditions, Refund & Cancellation Policy, Privacy Policy, Website Disclaimer Policy, and other applicable policies.

Where an inconsistency exists, the applicable written agreement and mandatory requirements of South African law will be taken into consideration.
