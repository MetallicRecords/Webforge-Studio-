Shipping & Delivery Policy

Information about the delivery of WebForge Studio digital services, projects, and related products.

Effective Date: [26 August 2026]
Last Updated: [26 August 2026]

1. Introduction

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how WebForge Studio delivers its digital services and, where applicable, physical products or materials.

WebForge Studio primarily provides digital services. Most services are therefore delivered electronically or through online access rather than by physical shipping.

2. Digital Service Delivery

Website design, website development, online stores, custom digital solutions, SEO services, maintenance, and other digital services are normally delivered online.

Depending on the project, delivery may take place by publishing the completed website, providing access to a website or platform, transferring files, providing credentials, or delivering other agreed digital materials.

3. Project Delivery

Project delivery timelines depend on the selected package, agreed scope, project complexity, customer requirements, and availability of the information required to complete the work.

Where a specific delivery date or estimated completion period has been agreed, WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to meet that timeframe.

4. Customer Delays

Project delivery may be delayed where the customer does not provide required information, content, approvals, payments, access credentials, product information, images, or other materials within the required timeframe.

Delays caused by the customer may result in the estimated completion date being adjusted.

5. Website Launch

A website may be considered delivered when the agreed website has been completed, made available for review, or published to the agreed hosting environment, depending on the project arrangement.

Domain and hosting availability may depend on third-party providers and are not always within the direct control of WebForge Studio.

6. Online Store Delivery

Online-store projects are delivered digitally according to the agreed project scope.

Product information, pricing, inventory, shipping information, payment configuration, customer accounts, and other store content supplied by the customer remain the customer's responsibility unless otherwise agreed.

WebForge Studio does not physically fulfil customer orders unless a separate fulfilment arrangement has been agreed.

7. Domains & Hosting

Where WebForge Studio assists with domain registration, hosting, SSL certificates, email hosting, or related services, delivery depends partly on the relevant third-party provider.

WebForge Studio is not responsible for delays caused by third-party registration, verification, provisioning, technical failures, outages, or service restrictions.

8. Digital Files & Assets

Where project files or other digital assets are included in the agreed service, they may be delivered through electronic transfer, cloud storage, website access, or another agreed digital method.

The specific files or assets supplied will depend on the applicable package or quotation.

9. Physical Products

WebForge Studio does not generally sell or ship physical products as part of its standard website and digital services.

If a project specifically includes a physical product, printed material, hardware, or another item requiring delivery, the applicable delivery method, cost, and timeframe will be communicated separately.

10. Delivery Costs

Standard digital delivery does not normally require a physical shipping fee.

Any third-party delivery, courier, printing, hardware, or other physical delivery costs will be communicated to the customer before the applicable service or product is supplied.

11. Third-Party Delivery Services

Where delivery depends on a third-party service, including hosting providers, domain registrars, payment providers, courier companies, software platforms, or other external services, the relevant provider's processing times and terms may apply.

12. Delivery Confirmation

Where appropriate, WebForge Studio may confirm delivery by email, website publication, digital transfer, platform access, or another electronic method.

Customers should review delivered work promptly and notify WebForge Studio of any material issue relating to the agreed scope.

13. Failed Digital Delivery

If a customer believes that agreed digital materials have not been received or cannot be accessed, the customer should contact WebForge Studio as soon as reasonably possible.

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to investigate and resolve delivery or access problems relating to services within its control.

14. Customer Access Information

Where access credentials or account information are required for delivery, the customer is responsible for providing accurate information and maintaining appropriate access to their own accounts.

Customers should keep login credentials secure and should not share confidential access information unnecessarily.

15. Completion & Acceptance

Delivery and completion are assessed according to the agreed project scope, quotation, package, and applicable approval process.

Customer feedback or requested changes after completion may be handled according to the applicable revisions, maintenance, or additional-work terms.

16. Delays Outside Our Control

WebForge Studio will not be responsible for unreasonable delays caused by circumstances outside its reasonable control, including major internet outages, hosting failures, domain-registration delays, third-party platform failures, power failures, natural disasters, or other circumstances beyond reasonable control.

17. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or limit any consumer rights that apply under South African law.

Where applicable, delivery and performance will be handled in accordance with the customer's legal rights and the agreed terms of the transaction.

18. Changes to This Policy

WebForge Studio may update this Shipping & Delivery Policy when necessary to reflect changes to our services, delivery methods, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

19. Governing Law

This policy is intended for WebForge Studio in South Africa and is subject to applicable South African law.
