1. Our Commitment to Accessibility

WebForge Studio is committed to making its website and digital services reasonably accessible and usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities or accessibility needs.

We aim to provide a clear, practical, and user-friendly online experience while continuing to improve accessibility as our website, technology, and services develop.

2. Accessibility and Inclusive Design

WebForge Studio recognises that people may access websites using different devices, browsers, assistive technologies, input methods, screen sizes, and accessibility settings.

We therefore aim to consider accessibility and inclusive design when developing and maintaining our website and digital solutions.

3. Accessible Website Experience

We aim to provide website content that is reasonably understandable, navigable, readable, and usable across supported devices and commonly used browsers.

Our accessibility efforts may include appropriate text structure, readable content, descriptive labels, usable navigation, responsive layouts, and consideration of keyboard and assistive-technology access where reasonably practicable.

4. Mobile and Responsive Accessibility

WebForge Studio designs its website with responsive behaviour in mind so that visitors can access content using mobile phones, tablets, laptops, desktops, and other supported screen sizes.

We recognise that accessibility and usability may vary depending on the visitor's device, operating system, browser, settings, and assistive technology.

5. Ongoing Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. WebForge Studio may review and improve its website and digital services when accessibility issues are identified or when new technologies and reasonable improvements become available.

We welcome constructive feedback that can help us identify areas where the user experience may be improved.

6. Navigation and Structure

WebForge Studio aims to organise website content using clear headings, logical sections, descriptive links, and consistent navigation so that visitors can more easily understand and move through the website.

7. Readable Content

We aim to present website information using clear language, suitable text sizing, appropriate spacing, and sufficient visual distinction between important content and the surrounding interface.

Where reasonably practicable, content will be structured so that it remains understandable across different screen sizes and viewing conditions.

8. Images and Alternative Text

Where appropriate, WebForge Studio aims to provide descriptive alternative text for meaningful images so that visitors using assistive technologies can better understand their purpose or content.

Decorative images may not require alternative text where they do not provide meaningful information.

9. Links and Interactive Elements

WebForge Studio aims to use descriptive link text and clearly identifiable interactive elements where reasonably practicable.

We also aim to ensure that important navigation and actions can be understood without relying solely on visual appearance.

10. Keyboard and Assistive Technology

Where reasonably practicable, WebForge Studio aims to support common keyboard navigation and assistive technologies used to access web content.

However, accessibility may vary depending on the specific browser, device, operating system, assistive technology, third-party software, and website feature being used.

11. Colour and Visual Presentation

WebForge Studio aims to maintain clear visual distinctions between text, backgrounds, buttons, links, and other important interface elements.

We aim not to rely solely on colour to communicate important information where a suitable alternative can reasonably be provided.

12. Forms and User Input

Where forms are provided on the WebForge Studio website, we aim to use clear labels, understandable instructions, and identifiable input fields where reasonably practicable.

Visitors should provide accurate information and may contact us if they experience difficulty completing a form or submitting an enquiry.

13. Documents and Downloadable Content

WebForge Studio may provide documents or other downloadable resources through its website.

We aim to make important documents reasonably accessible where practicable. However, some third-party documents, legacy materials, or externally supplied files may not fully meet the same accessibility standards.

14. Video, Audio, and Multimedia

Where multimedia content is used, WebForge Studio aims to consider accessibility features such as captions, transcripts, descriptive information, or alternative presentation methods where reasonably practicable.

Accessibility of embedded third-party multimedia may depend on the accessibility features provided by the relevant third-party platform.

15. Third-Party Services

The WebForge Studio website may contain links, integrations, tools, payment services, maps, social-media features, hosting services, or other technologies provided by third parties.

WebForge Studio does not control the accessibility features of third-party services and cannot guarantee their accessibility. Visitors may need to refer to the relevant third party for accessibility support.

16. Accessibility of Client Websites

Where WebForge Studio develops a website or digital solution for a client, accessibility requirements may be included within the agreed project scope.

The extent of accessibility work provided for a client project will depend on the agreed specifications, budget, technology, content, third-party services, and applicable requirements.

17. Client Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for providing accurate and suitable content, documents, images, videos, and other materials required for their projects.

Where accessibility depends on client-supplied material, WebForge Studio cannot guarantee accessibility of content that is supplied in an inaccessible or unsuitable format.

18. Accessibility Requests

Visitors who experience difficulty accessing information or using a feature of the WebForge Studio website are encouraged to contact us.

Where reasonably practicable, we will consider requests for alternative formats, clarification, or reasonable assistance.

19. Reporting an Accessibility Problem

If you identify an accessibility barrier, please provide enough information for WebForge Studio to understand the issue, including the affected page or feature and, where possible, the device or browser being used.

Reports may be reviewed to determine whether a reasonable correction or improvement can be made.

20. Reasonable Efforts

WebForge Studio will make reasonable efforts to improve accessibility within its technical and operational capabilities.

Accessibility improvements may be prioritised according to the severity of an issue, its impact on users, technical feasibility, available resources, and the nature of the affected service.

21. Accessibility and Website Performance

WebForge Studio recognises that website performance can affect accessibility and usability, particularly for visitors using mobile devices, slower connections, or limited resources.

Where reasonably practicable, we aim to consider performance, responsive behaviour, and efficient presentation of content when improving the website experience.

22. Browser and Device Compatibility

WebForge Studio aims to support commonly used modern browsers and devices where reasonably practicable.

We cannot guarantee identical functionality or presentation across every browser, device, operating system, screen configuration, or assistive technology.

23. Accessibility Limitations

Despite reasonable efforts, some parts of the website may not yet be fully accessible to every visitor.

Certain limitations may result from technical constraints, legacy content, third-party technology, external services, or materials supplied by clients or other parties.

24. Accessibility and Website Updates

Changes to website design, functionality, content, technology, or third-party services may temporarily affect accessibility or compatibility.

WebForge Studio will aim to address identified accessibility issues within a reasonable period where the issue is within our control and a reasonable correction is technically and operationally practicable.

25. No Guarantee of Universal Accessibility

WebForge Studio aims to provide an inclusive and accessible website experience but cannot guarantee that every page, feature, document, integration, or third-party service will be accessible to every person in every circumstance.

This statement does not limit any rights or protections that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable South African law.

26. Accessibility Feedback

WebForge Studio welcomes constructive feedback regarding the accessibility and usability of its website and digital services.

Feedback helps us identify barriers and determine where reasonable improvements may be made.

27. Assistance for Visitors

If you require assistance accessing information on the WebForge Studio website, you may contact us and explain the assistance you require.

We will consider reasonable requests and, where practicable, attempt to provide an appropriate alternative or explanation.

28. Accessibility and Privacy

Accessibility requests should contain only information reasonably necessary to help us address the accessibility issue.

Personal information provided when requesting accessibility assistance will be handled in accordance with the WebForge Studio Privacy Policy and applicable privacy legislation.

29. Accessibility and Security

Accessibility features must not compromise reasonable website security, protection of user information, or the integrity of WebForge Studio systems.

WebForge Studio may restrict or modify a feature where reasonably necessary to address a genuine security, fraud, technical, or legal concern.

30. Policy Review

WebForge Studio may review this Accessibility Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, website functionality, accessibility practices, business operations, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version published on this website will constitute the current version of this policy.

31. Governing Law

This Accessibility Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to any mandatory rights, protections, and remedies provided by applicable law.

32. Accessibility and Legal Rights

Nothing in this Accessibility Policy is intended to exclude, restrict, or remove any right or protection that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable South African law.
